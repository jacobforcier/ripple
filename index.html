How it works

Three steps.
Two of them are nothing.

Ripple only acts when you click it — there's nothing running in the background. Install it once, then open it whenever you want to share a product.

01

Install once

Add the Ripple extension to your browser. Takes about 30 seconds. You'll never think about it again.

02

Click Ripple to get your link

On a product you love, click the Ripple icon and copy your share link. Send it to your group chat, Slack, or text thread — wherever you'd normally share.

03

Send it to a friend

Paste your link wherever you'd normally share. If a friend buys through it, a small thank-you is set aside for you — but the point is making the recommendation easy.

The idea

Built for sharing, not for selling.

Ripple isn't a way to make money. It's a faster way to recommend things to friends — with a small thank-you when a recommendation lands, and a built-in disclosure so it's always honest.

Example

The board game

Questions

Good questions,
honest answers.

Do my friends know it's an affiliate link?

Yes. Every Ripple link redirects through a brief disclosure page that clearly states you may earn a small commission if they purchase. Transparency is built in — it's not optional.

Do I need to sign up for Amazon Associates or any affiliate programs?

No. Ripple handles all affiliate program relationships on the backend. You sign up for Ripple once, and every supported retailer is automatically covered.

Is this a way to make money?

No — and we want to be clear about that. The thank-you is usually cents to a couple of dollars on a purchase. Ripple is meant to make recommending things easier, not to be an income source. If you're looking for serious affiliate earnings, this isn't the tool for that.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Ripple is free to add and free to use. Your friends pay the normal retailer price — sharing through Ripple never changes what anything costs them.
